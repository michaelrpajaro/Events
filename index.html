MONDAY · KIDS GROUP CLASSES

For Ages 7 to 18

Coaching for young drivers, every Monday at 5 PM. Six rotating classes — Open Wheel, Sports Car, Street Car, Racecraft, Prototype, and Manual. Small groups, structured instruction, real laptime gains.

TUESDAY · GROUP DISCOUNT

Race Together. Save Together.

Roll in with 2 or more drivers on Tuesdays and save. 15% off before 4 PM, 10% off after 4 PM. And every weekday has its own promo — check the full list to find the deal that fits your visit.

See all daily promos →
WEDNESDAY · WORKSHOP WEDNESDAYS

Free Workshops

Every Wednesday at 8 PM. 20 minutes in the classroom on a rotating topic — Intro to Sim Racing, How to Drive Manual, or How to Drift — always free. Then hop on a sim and race with everyone (optional add-on, 20 or 40 min).

Book the next workshop →
THURSDAY · WEEKLY SPRINT RACE

Thursdays. 8 PM. Lights Out.

A one-hour race night: 10 min practice, 10 min qualifying, then a 40 min race. Tickets are $45 — a full hour of driving for $15 less than a standard hour. Same car and track as the current weekly qualifier (posted on our Instagram).

Reserve your spot →
FRIDAY · FRIDAY NIGHT SHOWDOWN

Compete Coast to Coast.

Fridays at 8 PM. A one-hour session for $60, racing against sim centers from across the US — 15 to 35 drivers on the grid, a different car category every week (GT, open-wheel, dirt, and more). Car and track drop on our Instagram a few days out. You'll need your own iRacing account and to own that week's content.

Book the Friday Night Showdown →
SATURDAY · CAR MEETS

Front Lot. Different Crew Each Time.

We've hosted Lamborghini Paramus, Tristate Motorclub, RIX Magazine, Daikoku NYC, and more. Meets take place outside in our front lot and run alongside sim racing tournaments inside, so there's something happening in both directions.

See upcoming meets on Instagram →
SUNDAY · FREE F1 WATCH PARTIES

Every Race. Free to Attend.

We host free watch parties for every race of the Formula 1 season. Multiple viewing areas anchored by a 230-inch LED screen, so no matter where you grab a seat you don't miss a corner.

SCALE DRIFT SERIES

Pro Tournaments. Public Demos.

In collaboration with the Scale Drift Series. Every few months, top RC drifters take over the floor for an all-day tournament — with breaks built in for the public to grab a controller and run free demo laps on the scale track.

Follow @scale_drift_series on Instagram →
